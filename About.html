About Me

I'm Ratnadeep, I build web apps automate workflows with Discord bots, and tinker with hardware.

I got into tech purely out of curiosity: taking things apart, figuring out how they work under the hood, and breaking code until I fixed it. That curiosity eventually turned into building real, functioning software from scratch.

Most of my time goes into full-stack web development, crafting clean, responsive interfaces and reliable backends. When I’m not writing web apps, I’m usually building custom Discord bots for online communities or wiring up microcontrollers and sensors for IoT projects.

For me, every project is a chance to pick up a new framework, solve a weird bug, and sharpen my skills.
